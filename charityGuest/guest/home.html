How FoodRescue Works

We bring donors, charities, and volunteers together to make food rescue simple.

01

Donate Surplus Food

Restaurants and food donors post their available surplus food with quantity, location, and expiry information.

02

Charities Claim Food

Verified charity organizations browse available food and claim donations before the expiry window.

03

Volunteers Deliver

Volunteer riders collect the food and deliver it safely to the receiving charity organization.

04

People Are Served

Charities distribute rescued food and record the number of people served.

Why FoodRescue?

Turning Food Waste Into Community Impact.

Every day, perfectly usable food can go to waste while people in our communities face food insecurity. FoodRescue creates a simple connection between surplus food and organizations that can put it to good use.

Reduce unnecessary food waste
Connect donors with verified charities
Make pickups and handovers traceable
Track meals rescued and people served

Rescue Before It Goes to Waste

A surplus meal may only have a few hours before it becomes unusable. FoodRescue helps organizations act within that critical window.

1 Meal 1 Less Waste 1 More Person Served
