Food Safety & Guidelines

Follow these guidelines to ensure that donated food remains safe, fresh and suitable for distribution.

Food Safety & Expiry

Fresh

Food that is within its safe consumption period and has been stored under appropriate conditions.

  • Check the preparation and expiry date.
  • Keep food properly covered and stored.
  • Maintain the recommended storage temperature.

Expiring Soon

Food that is approaching its expiry or use-by date should be prioritized for collection and distribution.

  • Clearly check the expiry or use-by date.
  • Prioritize timely pickup and distribution.
  • Follow the required storage conditions.

Expired

Food that has passed its expiry or use-by date must not be accepted for donation or distribution.

  • Do not donate expired food.
  • Remove expired food from active listings.
  • Do not distribute food that may be unsafe.

Food Handling Guidelines

1

Check Food Condition

Food should be free from visible contamination, spoilage, unusual odor or damaged packaging.

2

Maintain Proper Storage

Store food according to its required temperature and storage conditions. Temperature-sensitive food should not be left at room temperature unnecessarily.

3

Use Safe Packaging

Food should be placed in clean, suitable and properly covered containers to prevent contamination.

4

Ensure Timely Pickup

Temperature-sensitive food should be collected by the stated pickup time to maintain food quality and safety.

5

Maintain Hygiene

Anyone handling donated food should maintain proper personal hygiene and use clean equipment and transportation containers.

6

Provide Accurate Information

Food listings should include accurate information about food type, quantity, preparation date, expiry date and storage requirements where applicable.

Compliance & Agreement

All participants involved in food donation, collection and distribution are expected to follow these food safety guidelines.

FoodRescue reserves the right to reject food that appears expired, contaminated or unsuitable for safe distribution.
